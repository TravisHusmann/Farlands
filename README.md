# Farlands

Top-down pixel-art action RPG built in **Unity 6**.

You wash up on a small isle at the edge of a broken world and cross a
continent of five zones — Verdant, Rime, the Hollow, Ashfall and the Breach —
each higher level than the last, toward the crater at its heart. Walled
cities are safe; the roads between them are not. Single-player for now.

## Download

**[Download the latest Windows build](https://github.com/TravisHusmann/Farlands/releases/latest)** — unzip anywhere and run `Farlands.exe`. To update, download the new zip and replace the old folder.

Characters and settings are kept in
`%USERPROFILE%\AppData\LocalLow\Farlands\Farlands\`. Updating never touches
them; deleting that folder resets everything.

## Controls

Two keyboard layouts, chosen at the top of the Controls page. Every key can be
rebound, and each layout keeps its own.

| Action | WASD layout | Click-to-move layout | Controller |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Move | `W A S D` | Right click (hold to follow the cursor) | Left stick |
| Aim | Mouse | Mouse | Skills aim at your target |
| Basic attack | Hold left mouse | Hold left mouse | Right trigger |
| Dodge | `Space` | `Space` | LB |
| Skills | `Q` `E` `R` `F` `Z` `X` `C` `V` | `Q` `W` `E` `R` `A` `S` `D` `F` | A, B, X, Y; hold LT for 5–8 |
| Ultimate | `F` | `F` | Y |
| Next target | `Tab` | `Tab` | Flick the right stick |
| Lock onto target | — | — | Hold RB |
| Health potion | `1` | `1` | D-pad down |
| Talk / use | `G` | `G` | D-pad left |
| Map | `M` | `M` | D-pad up |
| Inventory | `I` | `I` | D-pad right |
| Journal | `J` | `J` | Pause menu |
| Pause menu | `Esc` | `Esc` | Start |

## What's in it

One class, the Drifter: a pool of pistol and rifle skills, eight on the bar
at a time, with cancel windows and linking. The pistols fire at everything
around you as you move, a style rank rewards mixing your skills up and
quickens your step, and
Momentum pays for the heavy pistol skills and charges the ultimate. A world
built from its own seed and kept that way: a continent of regions, walled
cities — no two laid out alike — and roadside villages joined by winding
roads, bridges and passes, signposts at every junction. Each zone has its own
creatures, each with its own attacks, taking turns to strike and growing more
dangerous zone by zone, with a hard step up from Ashfall on; camps that wake as
a pack; and a mini-boss in its lair. Under Ashfall, a smoking pit off the road
leads down three floors -- a prison, a burning forge, a temple of bone -- to the
Gatekeeper and the gate to Hell: seven circles, from a black river to a frozen
lake where Hell's lord waits, and below them depths without end. At the Breach's heart lies the crater the
AI broke out of, the world itself torn open at its middle; beat what hunts you
at level 60 and its wound lets you through, into what is left where there is
no world at all. Something else hunts
the whole world, learns how you fight, and remembers you; only a city gate
stops it. Towns and roads full of people, livestock and wildlife; weather and
a day and night; an illustrated world map with fog of war, a minimap, portals
between the cities you have discovered, and leveling to 60. Gear in nine
places, from plain to legendary -- legendaries that change a skill, each
mini-boss's own unique -- picked up by walking over it; a Blacksmith whose
upgrades never fail, a Codex that keeps every power you salvage, a bank stash
shared by all your characters, and food and elixirs for the way down. The
story is told in what you find: letters, tally stones and records lying
where the world broke, a drowned hunter's notes on everything you kill, and
feats that earn titles. Side quests from townsfolk and notice boards, two
delves in every zone with a legendary beast at the bottom, and a forecast for
the weather; nights that pay more and bring riders, night creatures and the
odd Glitch Night. Skill variants from level 46 that change how a skill plays.
Gold worth spending -- prices that grow with you, a Curio Dealer, bigger packs
-- and cosmetics earned in play: coat dyes, companions, shot styles,
footprints, banners where you have won, portrait frames, and a home to
furnish with the bosses' trophies. Waterfalls, lit candelabras and the rest of
Pixel World's effects.
Characters and worlds are saved separately, and a new world can be Tiny,
Small, Medium, Large or Huge.

Still to come: more classes; the main quest; multiplayer, where you host a
world and friends join it, with open-world PvP the host can switch on or off.
